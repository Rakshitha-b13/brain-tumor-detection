🔐 Privacy-Aware Architecture

These results demonstrate the performance trade-off associated with privacy-aware collaborative learning. The federated approach enables distributed model training across participating clients without directly centralizing their local training images.

Patient Data Privacy & Protection

Privacy safeguards applied during MRI analysis

🛡 No Patient Identification

The application does not request or display patient names, phone numbers, addresses, or personal identification numbers.

🧠 MRI-Based Analysis

The AI analysis is performed using the submitted MRI image and does not require personal patient information.

💻 Local Prediction History

Prediction history is stored locally in the browser and contains the prediction, confidence, time, and report reference.

🔄 Federated Privacy

Federated training avoids directly centralizing the participating clients' local training images.

Privacy note: No patient-identifying fields are collected by this research prototype. Uploaded MRI files are processed by the application for analysis and report generation. The prototype should not be used as a production electronic medical-record system.
